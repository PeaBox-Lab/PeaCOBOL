# Changelog

## 0.5.0 — Assurance and Change Impact

### Added

- Resolved program call graph and copybook dependency graph.
- Field read/write, paragraph control-flow, and supported external-input data-flow inventories.
- Bounds, truncation, sensitive-data-flow, and `MOVE CORRESPONDING` assurance rules.
- Field-level copybook change-impact comparison with compatibility classification and integrity hashes.
- SARIF 2.1.0 export.
- Configurable severity and breaking-change gates through `assure` and `impact`.
- Non-downgradable security controls for release-oriented profiles.
- Symlink-safe contained build targets and serialized audit append/verification transactions.

### Changed

- Version advanced from 0.4.0 to 0.5.0.

## 0.4.0 — Diagnostics Center

- Spool analysis, validated local diagnostic catalogs, source correlation, deterministic investigation checklists, and hashed failure bundles.

## 0.3.0 — Build Profiles and IBM JCL

- Versioned profiles, IBM JCL generation, normalized compiler diagnostics, build manifests, representation controls, and policy gates.

## 0.2.0 — Data Integrity and Audit Workbench

- Hierarchical copybooks, fixed-record tools, reproducible evidence, hash-chained auditing, and safer local operation.

## 0.1.0

- Initial project manager, analyzer, copybook inspector, local UI, GnuCOBOL adapter, evidence generator, sample, and tests.
